# Source and inspiration

This skill's source-writing principles are inspired by Diomidis Spinellis,
especially his section
"Write readable and maintainable LaTeX source code"
in [Advice for writing LaTeX documents](https://github.com/dspinellis/latex-advice#write-readable-and-maintainable-latex-source-code).
They are adapted here into operational guidance for an editing agent.

This note records skill provenance and is intentionally not linked from
`SKILL.md` or loaded during ordinary skill use.
